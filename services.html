Services

Seven kinds of session.

Studio and location work across Nairobi, quoted on what the day actually needs.

Services

What a session actually includes.

Every shoot is quoted on what it needs — how long, how many looks, studio or location, photo only or photo and video. Send the details and you’ll get a straight number back.

Studio Portraits

Controlled light, seamless backdrops and as many looks as the session allows. This is the bread and butter — most of the portfolio starts here.

  • Seamless colour backdrops
  • Coloured gels
  • Hard-light setups
  • Multiple outfit changes
Ask about Studio Portraits

Weddings

Full-day coverage, from preparations through to the reception, photo and video. Trains, veils and beadwork get shot for the detail, not just the wide.

  • Full-day coverage
  • Photo and video
  • Couple portraits
  • Detail and decor
Ask about Weddings

Graduation Shoots

Cap, gown, stole and the people who got you there. Booked heavily around the Nairobi university calendar, so early dates go first.

  • Solo and family
  • Gown and stole detail
  • Coloured-gel sets
  • Group bookings
Ask about Graduation Shoots

Maternity & Couples

Bump shoots and couple sessions, in studio or on location. Wardrobe and posing directed on the day — nobody is left to work out what to do with their hands.

  • Maternity
  • Couples and pre-wedding
  • Studio or outdoor
  • Posing directed
Ask about Maternity & Couples

Birthdays & Celebrations

Milestone shoots built around a set — cake, balloons, a gobo on the backdrop. Studio-built or covered live at the venue.

  • Milestone sets
  • Cake and decor
  • On-location cover
  • Baby and child
Ask about Birthdays & Celebrations

Outdoor & Location

Parks, bridges, gardens and the streets around Nairobi. Natural light worked properly rather than fought, and available across Kenya on request.

  • Natural light
  • Nairobi locations
  • Travel on request
  • Golden-hour sets
Ask about Outdoor & Location

Corporate & Brand

Headshots, team portraits and product or brand imagery — consistent framing and colour so a full set actually looks like a set.

  • Headshots
  • Team portraits
  • Brand and product
  • Consistent colour
Ask about Corporate & Brand
