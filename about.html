The studio

One photographer, and a room in Kasarani.

Extrah Studio is run by Quinton Wachira — creative director, editor, colourist and the person actually behind the camera on the day.

The studio sits in Clay City, Kasarani. Most sessions happen there, on seamless backdrops, with light shaped rather than sprayed. The rest happen wherever the shoot calls for: a garden after dark, a stone staircase, a bridge off Thika Road.

The work is mostly portraits — graduations, birthdays, maternity, couples, brand and model portfolios — plus weddings covered for both photo and video. Bookings are taken across Nairobi and, on request, the rest of Kenya.

Because Quinton edits and grades everything himself, a set comes back looking like a set: same colour, same treatment, start to finish.
